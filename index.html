💳 UPI Scam Awareness

📲

Receive Money QR Scam

Scammers send a QR code claiming "scan to receive money." Scanning it actually debits your account. Never scan unknown QR codes.

HIGH RISK
🏦

Fake KYC Update Calls

Fraudsters pose as bank/UPI officials asking you to share OTP or install remote-access apps (AnyDesk, TeamViewer) to "update KYC." Banks never ask for OTPs.

HIGH RISK
🎁

Cashback / Reward Fraud

Fake messages promise cashback but ask you to send ₹1 first to "verify your account." The small payment leads to larger unauthorized debits.

MEDIUM RISK
🔗

Phishing Payment Links

Links like "pay.upi-claim-reward.xyz" mimic genuine UPI pages. Always verify the payment URL and recipient VPA before authorizing any transaction.

HIGH RISK
📞

Screen Share Fraud

Callers claiming to be customer support ask you to share your screen. This gives them visibility of your PIN and banking apps in real time.

HIGH RISK
💰

Send ₹1 to Collect ₹1000

Classic advance-fee fraud adapted for UPI. No legitimate service requires a small payment to release a larger reward. Ignore and report.

MEDIUM RISK
